How to Play
1

You'll see 16 NBA players in a grid. They secretly belong to 4 groups of 4, each sharing a hidden connection — a team, an era, a stat, a nickname, anything.

2

Tap 4 players you think belong together, then hit Submit. Guess wrong and one of your mistakes is used up.

3

You get 4 mistakes total before the puzzle ends. Solve all 4 groups before you run out to win.

4

Each player card shows their jersey number — sometimes a clue, sometimes just a fun fact.
