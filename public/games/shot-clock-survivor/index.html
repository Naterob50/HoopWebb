How to Play

437 Questions
⏱
Beat the shot clock Each question comes with 4 answer choices. Pick the right one before the orange ring drains to zero — or you lose a life.
⚡
Speed = more points Answer fast to score big. You earn up to 6 bonus points for answering in the first 2 seconds. Slow answers still score, but only 1 point.
🔴
Three lives Wrong answer or clock expires = lose a life. Lose all three and the game ends. There are no extra lives.
📈
Level up — clock gets shorter Correct answers earn XP. Fill the bar to level up — but every two levels the shot clock drops. At level 7+ you only get 12 seconds.
