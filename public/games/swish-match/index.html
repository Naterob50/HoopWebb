How to Play

Goal: Swap two adjacent pieces to match 3 or more of the same kind. Reach the target score before your moves run out to clear the level and advance.

Controls: Tap a piece, then tap an adjacent piece to swap them. A swap that creates a match happens; otherwise the pieces shake and swap back — no move is wasted.

Power-Ups

Power-ups unlock gradually as you level up, so you can learn the basics first:

  • ➡ Fast Break (unlocks Level 10) — match 4 in a row or column to clear that whole line.
  • ✚ Slam Dunk (unlocks Level 25) — match an L or T shape to blast a 3×3 area.
  • ▦ Full Press (unlocks Level 40) — match a 2×2 square to clear its row and column.
  • ★ Buzzer Beater (unlocks Level 60) — match 5 in a line to create a wild star. Swap it with any piece to clear every piece of that type — swap two together to clear the whole board!
  • ✛ Alley-Oop (unlocks Level 80) — match 6 or more in a line for a big plus-shaped blast.

Tips

Combos: Chain reactions multiply your score — each cascading match after the first scores more than the last, so set up multi-step chains when you can.

Moves & Levels: Every level has a fixed move budget and target score. Run out of moves first and you'll retry the exact same puzzle with a fresh set of moves. A live timer tracks how long each attempt takes — your best completion times are saved to your personal leaderboard alongside your score.

Use Pause to step away, Quit to bail back to Level 1, and Sound to toggle audio — all available anytime from the buttons below the board.

🌀 Zen Mode: No timer, no move limit, every power-up unlocked from the start. Keep matching on an endless board until no matches remain — chase your highest possible score. Switch to it anytime with the Zen Mode button.

💡 Hint: Stuck? Tap Hint to glow-highlight a valid move. You get 3 per level (5 in Zen Mode) — they refresh on Retry or a new level.
