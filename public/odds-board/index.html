What Is The Odds Board?

The Odds Board is a real-time NBA betting intelligence platform built for sharp viewers who want clean, fast access to lines, spreads, totals, and player props without the clutter of a sportsbook interface.

We pull live data from 35+ licensed sportsbooks via The Odds API, updating every 60 seconds so you always see where the market is moving before tip-off.

What We Are Not

We are not a sportsbook. We do not accept wagers. We do not offer picks, touts, or gambling advice. The Odds Board is purely an informational tool — think of it as a Bloomberg terminal for NBA lines.

Data Sources

All odds data is sourced via The Odds API (the-odds-api.com), aggregating lines from DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, PointsBet, and 30+ other licensed US sportsbooks in real time.
